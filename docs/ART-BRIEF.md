# Sticky Molecules: art brief for ChatGPT

Game: **Sticky Molecules**, an ItsDrDan chemistry study game on intermolecular forces, phase changes and colligative properties (CHEM 3A, 1A, 3B). Repo: ItsDrDan/IMF.

Please make the images in this file one at a time, in the order listed. Keep the same character and the same style in every image.

---

## 1. House style (apply to every image)

Flat, friendly vector illustration for a chemistry study game badge. Thick dark-navy outlines (#2e2b38), soft pastel fills (mint #e8f6ef, lavender #f3effd, sky #eaf4fb, peach #fdeee8, butter #fff6e2), one warm accent (#ffd36b), subtle paper texture, no gradients heavier than a light wash, **no text, no letters, no numbers**, no photorealism, no 3D render. Centered subject, generous margin, transparent background.

## 2. Chemistry rules (please follow exactly; students learn from these pictures)

- **Water (H₂O)** is bent, about 104.5°, never a straight line. One larger oxygen (soft coral/peach) with two smaller hydrogens (cream/white).
- The oxygen carries **two lone pairs**, drawn as two small pairs of dots on the side away from the hydrogens.
- A **hydrogen bond** is a **dashed** line (short dashes), drawn from an H on one molecule to a lone pair on the oxygen of another molecule, roughly in a straight line. **Never a solid line**: solid lines mean real covalent bonds.
- Molecules never break apart in any picture. Steam is still whole H₂O molecules, just farther apart.
- A **red blood cell** is a biconcave disc, like a donut with a dent instead of a hole.
- **Fatty acid tails** are zigzag lines. A cis double bond puts a visible kink in the chain.
- No δ+ / δ− symbols, since there is no text. Show partial charges as a soft glow instead: light blue near the hydrogens, light pink near the oxygen.

## 3. Game logo (1 image)

Size: 1024 × 1024, round badge composition with a thin gold ring (#e1b072) and a navy outer ring.

Subject: two cheerful cartoon water molecules, each with a small friendly face on the oxygen, leaning toward each other. A glowing **dashed** hydrogen bond links the hydrogen of one molecule to a lone pair on the other's oxygen. A small honey-like drip runs off the bottom of the badge to suggest "sticky". Tiny sparkles around the dashed bond.

Save as: `imf.webp` (hub) and `logo.png` (512 × 512, for the repo).

## 4. Mascot: "Ollie" the water molecule (7 images)

Ollie is one cartoon water molecule: a round coral oxygen body with a face, two cream hydrogen "ears/arms" set at the bent angle, and two small lone-pair dots on top like antennae. Short stubby feet. Same character, same proportions, same colors in every mood. Each mood is a separate 1024 × 1024 image on a transparent background, no badge ring.

| # | Mood | Pose and expression |
|---|------|---------------------|
| 1 | idle | Friendly smile, waving with one hydrogen arm |
| 2 | thinking | Looking up, one hydrogen arm on chin, small question-mark-shaped *dashed* line above (no text) |
| 3 | correct | Big grin, both hydrogen arms up, a dashed H-bond "high five" connecting to a small partner molecule |
| 4 | oops | Worried eyebrows, sweat drop, small shrug, kind not sad |
| 5 | victory | Jumping, confetti, holding a tiny gold medal |
| 6 | heated | Sweating and jittering (motion lines), steam puffs around, still smiling; for the temperature slider |
| 7 | chilly | Shivering, frost on the edges, sitting in a small hexagonal ice-lattice frame; for freezing |

Save as: `mascot-idle.png`, `mascot-thinking.png`, `mascot-correct.png`, `mascot-oops.png`, `mascot-victory.png`, `mascot-heated.png`, `mascot-chilly.png`.
