## B. Chemistry corrections (students learn from these pictures)

1. **Ollie must be bent in every mood.** Both hydrogens (the cream "hands") sit on the **lower half** of the body, about 104.5° apart, like a wide "V" opening upward toward the lone pairs. In idle, correct and oops, one hand is high and one is low, which makes the molecule look nearly straight. Keep both hands low and symmetric.
2. **Lone pairs:** draw **two pairs** of dots on top (four dots total, as two little pairs), not two single dots.
3. **A hydrogen bond goes from an H on one molecule to a lone pair on the oxygen of the other molecule, never H to H.** Fix these:
   - **logo:** the dashed line should start at the right-hand H of the left molecule and end at a lone-pair dot on top of the right molecule's oxygen (tilt the right molecule so its lone pairs face the left molecule).
   - **mascot-correct:** the "high five" currently touches hand to hand (H to H). Make the partner molecule's lone pair meet Ollie's hand with a short dashed line instead.
   - **first-stick:** same fix: H of one molecule → dashed line → lone pair of the other. Not H to H.
   - **hbond-hero:** every dashed line must run from an H to a lone pair. Show the center molecule using its own two H's (to two neighbors' lone pairs) and its own lone pairs (receiving two neighbors' H's). Four neighbors total, all fully inside the badge.
4. **dipole-duo:** the two molecules are touching hand to hand (H to H). Line them up so the light-blue (H, δ+) end of one faces the pink (O, δ−) end of the other, with a small gap and **no line** between them (a dipole–dipole attraction, not a bond).
5. **ion-hugger:** good: oxygens point toward the ion. Just keep all the water molecules fully inside the badge.
6. **fat-packer:** show at least three straight zigzag chains stacked neatly on top, and below them two chains that each have a clear bend (kink) in the middle so they cannot stack.
7. **protein-folder:** show the helix **and** a pleated (zigzag) sheet next to it, with a few short dashed lines between the turns of the helix (backbone hydrogen bonds).
