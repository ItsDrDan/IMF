What osmotic pressure means

Water moves through the membrane into the solution. The osmotic pressure, Π, is the pressure on the piston that just stops that net flow. Π = iMRT, with T in kelvin.

This is a teaching model. Solute concentrations are relative to the inside of a normal red blood cell (1.0, the same as 0.9% NaCl). The water counts are relative rates, not real numbers of molecules. Solute particles cannot cross the membrane; water can.
